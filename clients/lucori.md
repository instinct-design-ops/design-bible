# Lucori

Client rules in force for Lucori (spelled "Lucorri" in the Figma file name). Read this file together with design-bible.md before any Lucori work. The review rounds behind these rules are history/rejected-directions.md entries 1 to 3. Approved mood-board directions and links to the approved work are in reference/approved-directions.md.

## Brand facts

Read from the client's file, 20 Nov 2026. These are the brand's own constraints and hold whatever the composition.

- **LU-01** · Headline: Newsreader serif, with one honey-coloured italic accent word or line. (This is the brand's own system, so the one-family headline rule in design-bible.md L-24 does not override it for Lucori.)
- **LU-02** · Body: Inter.
- **LU-03** · CTA: black pill.
- **LU-04** · Ground: cream.
- **LU-05** · The "Lucori" wordmark appears on every ad.
- **LU-06** · Imagery: humans only, warm everyday lifestyle, real skin, premium-stock look. No AI slop.
- **LU-07** · Disclaimer microcopy sits at the bottom edge, per the MPS, on medical assets.

## From the rejected rounds (20-21 Nov 2026)

- **LU-08** · The honey italic accent appears on both V1 and V2 (it was missing on V1 in the rejected set).
- **LU-09** · Cream ground without the template: no cream card with a photo strip. Either a full-bleed photo with type in the quiet zone, or a confident solid ground with one staged element (design-bible.md V-05).
