## Headline weight contrast with the red pay-off on light grounds (25 Nov 2026, round 2)

Original text:

This replaces the 24 Nov idea of an italic serif phrase as the dark-ground emphasis for Alpha One.

Replaced by: 25 Nov 2026, round 3: one emphasis device per headline. On light grounds the red key phrase is the emphasis and the headline stays one weight; weight contrast is only for all-white headlines on dark grounds. The one-family part still holds.
