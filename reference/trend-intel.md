# Trend intel playbook

What the best designers, agencies and award-winning ads are doing now, kept by the daily trend-intel job (Job T) since 28 Nov 2026, from Shresht: "Whenever you design an ad in the future, you always use those points ... Never work as a stock." Read it before concepting any ad (W-15).

How it works:
- Each move is one line: the move, where it was seen (work, maker, proof), the sighting count and the last date seen.
- **In force:** seen 3+ times from at least 2 different top sources (an award, Best of Behance, a roster agency or designer). Reach for these first.
- **Watching:** seen once or twice. Use one only when it clearly fits the brief.
- **Fading:** not seen for 6 weeks. Don't lead with it.
- **Clashes with a rule:** a move that would break a Bible rule or one of Shresht's rejections. Logged so it isn't rediscovered, never used until he says so.
- At most 25 moves. Daily finds go in research-feed.md and expire after 2 days (H-09); this file is what lasts. A move becomes a numbered Bible rule only on Shresht's word.
- Visual references, never assets to copy. Brand rules, the client file and the brief always win.

## Ideas and devices

- **Miniature world on the body** (watching, 2, last 28 Nov 2026): a tiny built world on the skin or body part where the product works, the product at real scale. HUAWEI FreeClip arena in the ear (multiple owners, Behance, 394 appreciations); Vaseline miniature repair crew on cracked skin (Saifullah Sardar, Behance, 170). Fits wearables, skincare, recovery.
- **Aftermath, not action** (watching, 1, last 28 Nov 2026): show the evidence the problem left behind, not the problem happening; product small in the sign-off. Purina One "Hunger made him do it" (Malak Haredy, Behance most appreciated this week, 256). Works with zero humans (R-01).
- **The benefit floods the ordinary room** (watching, 1, last 28 Nov 2026): a real everyday room whose floor is taken over by the benefit's world. Oculus VR "Live the game" (Re Bo, Behance, 362).
- **One metaphor, three executions** (watching, 1, last 28 Nov 2026): one idea run as three different layout devices (scene, portrait, document) with one line and sign-off. Purina One (Malak Haredy, Behance). Distinct concepts for V-03, one campaign for V-01.
