# Style directions

Approved style directions and the research behind them, synced from the internal Design Bible Doc on 25 Nov 2026. The rule in force is in design-bible.md (V-09); this page is the source and the reference set. Visual references only, never assets to copy.

## Style direction: retro-UI sticker cards (approved by Shresht, 24 Nov 2026)

- 24 Nov 2026, from Shresht. He watched the signalservice.co Sellbird promo reel on Instagram and said "use it". Approved as a standing style direction for static ads (static-only, not a video brief).
- 24 Nov 2026. Ground: cream background with a faint dot grid. The grid adds texture without noise; keep it faint enough that text readability never dips.
- 24 Nov 2026. Cards: flat color fills with hard black offset shadows - a solid black shadow, shifted, no blur. Every card in a frame casts its shadow in the same direction.
- 24 Nov 2026. Sticker labels: bold short labels ("FREE", "19 NEED YOU") set on a slight tilt as accents over the cards. One or two stickers per frame, never a pile.
- 24 Nov 2026. Palette: orange, black and white as the base, with blue, green and purple as small accents. Accents stay small so the base trio owns the frame.
- 24 Nov 2026. A retro-UI tab bar with the current step highlighted works as a progress device inside the ad: it shows where the viewer is in a sequence without extra copy.
- 24 Nov 2026. Copy discipline in this style: one short caption pill per frame, and one idea with one big headline per frame. The pill carries the sentence; the headline carries the idea.
- 24 Nov 2026, daily layout research (drop 2): reference set for applying the retro-UI sticker card direction above.
- 24 Nov 2026. NN/g field guide to neobrutalism: the style reduced to rules - high contrast, thick borders, solid single-color offset shadows (about 4px, zero blur), bold quirky type balanced by whitespace, and Windows 98-style UI nostalgia. Its warning matches his readability rule: bold color pairs still have to pass text contrast. Source: nngroup.com/articles/neobrutalism
- 24 Nov 2026. UIDrops brutalist component set: the whole language is two rules - a 2px black border and a solid offset shadow with zero blur; pressed and hover states just translate the element into its own shadow. Stickers, washi tape, marker highlights and halftone surfaces are the accent kit. Use as the spec sheet when building the sticker cards. Source: uidrop.dev/components/brutalist
- 24 Nov 2026. SLAB Strength Club concept: neo-brutalism as brand voice - 3px borders with an 8px hard shadow, a logo made of three stacked type stamps with no photo in the hero, and pricing copy that reads "pay for iron, not interior design". Lesson: the style is an attitude; the copy has to talk the same way the cards look. Source: abbod.de/projects/slab-strength
- 24 Nov 2026. POSTER EXPO / BRUTAL Vol. I (2026): monochrome neo-brutalist posters playing strict geometric grids against raw scale. Layout reference for keeping sticker-card frames on a real grid instead of letting decorations float. Source: behance.net/gallery/252020343/POSTER-EXPO-BRUTAL-Vol-I
- 25 Nov 2026, daily layout research (drop 3): real brands running this style, as application references.
- 25 Nov 2026. O.P.I "What's Your Mani-tude?" (Fall 2025, Michael Burk Studio): a full 360 campaign built on custom branded stickers - the same sticker system runs across socials, OOH posters, packaging and displays. Lesson: one sticker kit, designed once, can carry every surface of a campaign. Source: michaelburk.studio/work/opi-whats-your-manitude
- 25 Nov 2026. Gumroad storefront (live): neo-brutalism selling to buyers, not designers - thick black borders, flat pink and yellow blocks, one giant first line ("Go from zero to $1"). Lesson: the style survives real commerce when the headline stays the biggest thing on the frame. Source: gumroad.com
- 25 Nov 2026. Tony's Chocolonely e-commerce by Tinloof: the chocolate bar's own grid becomes the layout device, with bold quirky type carrying the brand voice. Lesson: let the product's native geometry set the grid instead of inventing one. Source: tonyschocolonely.tinloof.com
- 25 Nov 2026. Figma brand refresh (Figma blog, Sep 2024): a system of simple primitives plus a four-font type stack (Sans, Condensed, Mono, Hand) generates endless compositions. Lesson: a small kit of shapes and a fixed type stack scales further than decorating each frame. Source: figma.com/blog/figma-on-figma-evolving-our-visual-language/
